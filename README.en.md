## Hi, I'm Yixiao (一笑)

I was trained in sociology and social data analysis, and I currently build AI products. Most of my work involves AIGC (AI-generated content) commercialization, content production, model output evaluation, and workflow design. I also use AI in my own research, writing, coding, and knowledge work.

I'm slowly turning real problems from my work — and the judgment behind them — into products and workflows that others can use, evaluate, and reuse. I'm still figuring things out, and I plan to gradually open up the parts that are ready to share.

## What I'm Focused On

- **AI-native interaction**: how to design interactions that only make sense with AI in the loop, letting users explore, collaborate, revise, and keep moving a task forward.
- **Commercializing AIGC tools**: how to bring image and video generation into content production and advertising pipelines, so generated output goes from a one-off experiment to a tool people rely on.
- **AI products in real industries**: how to pick real scenarios in advertising, content, and retail, organize data and evaluation around them, and build products that actually get used.
- **AI in physical business**: how to connect AI to stores, products, services, and operations, so that efficiency, conversion, or delivery changes in concrete ways.
- **Productizing AI workflows**: how to turn personal ways of working with AI into workflows that others can understand, run, and reuse.

## Open Work

I try to organize what can be public into materials others can open, understand, and reuse. Two projects are actively maintained for now; workflow templates, evaluation materials, and case studies are being prepared gradually.

- **[video-creative-agent](https://github.com/esmile-collab/video-creative-agent)**: organizes script, visual, and narrative patterns from industry videos, turning project experience into searchable, composable knowledge assets.
- **[resume-agent](https://github.com/esmile-collab/resume-agent)**: connects resume matching, generation, section-level polishing, and export into one AI-assisted workflow.

## What I've Done

### AIGC commercialization products

At Bilibili's commercialization business, I participated in ad headline, cover image, and video generation products. I was responsible for product iteration of specific modules, generation rules, triaging online issues, and industry validation — working continuously on how generated content gets understood, adopted, and integrated into production workflows.

### LLM content evaluation

At Tencent WeChat, I participated in data strategy and quality evaluation for an Official Accounts content summarization project. I helped turn factual accuracy, information coverage, citation traceability, and readability into checkable standards, and worked on sample design, quality inspection, human evaluation, and issue attribution.

### AIGC stock asset workflow

Around Adobe Stock's purchase scenarios and review rules, I built a stock-asset pipeline covering topic selection, prompting, batch generation, post-processing, and tag organization — continuously observing how generation capability fits with demand judgment, production organization, and platform rules.
